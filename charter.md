## PkCEP Charter

+ Diverse PkCEP aims to be diverse, actively engaging underrepresented demographics

+ Inclusive PkCEP will aim to be inclusive, reducing barriers to entry

+ Accessible PkCEP will aim to be accessible to everyone 
    + Always conducted in a hybrid mode; online participation will always be free
    + Where speakers permit, slides and video recordings will be made available freely and publicly
    + Authors of peer-reviewed work will be encouraged to make pre-prints available for free access

+ PkCEP will aim to be Open, Honest, Critical, and Constructive
