# 2nd Conference on Computing Education Practice in Pakistan

Welcome to the 2nd Annual Conference on Computing Education Practice in Pakistan, which will be held on **8th January 2027** *(tentative)*.

{% include about-conf-series-text.md %}

## Conference Venue

The conference will be hosted by [**University of Management and Technology, Lahore**](https://www.umt.edu.pk/)

**Address:**

University of Management and Technology C-II, 
Johar Town, Lahore.
[Map Link](https://www.google.com/maps?cid=1096969140280024454&g_mp=CiVnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLkdldFBsYWNlEAMYASAF&hl=en-GB&source=embed)

## Hybrid Event
The workshop will be conducted in a hybrid form. To encourage wider dissemination and inclusive access, all talks will be recorded and shared publicly after the workshop, subject to permission from the relevant speakers.

## Program

This is going to be a day-long conference, and the current tentative date for the conference is 8th January, 2007. The conference is expected to last from 9am to 4pm.
