# Conference 2026

## 13-15 July 2026 | Madrid, Spain

Welcome to the 31st Annual Conference on Innovation and Technology in Computer Science Education. This year's conference will take place in the vibrant city of Madrid, hosted by Universidad Rey Juan Carlos.

## Welcome to Madrid

Madrid is the capital of Spain, founded in the IX century as a Muslim settlement called Magerit. It was conquered in 1075 and integrated into the Kingdom of León, and later the Kingdom of Castile. In the XVI century, King Philip II established a stable royal court here, and since then it has experienced continuous growth.

Currently, Madrid has over 3,400,000 inhabitants, and the surrounding region has a population of about 7 million. It is a very active city, both as an industrial and financial center, as well as a cultural and bohemian hub.

### Getting to Madrid

Madrid is in the center of Spain and is very well connected with the rest of Spain and Portugal. It is the center of the Spanish railway network and a radial system of national highways.

- **By Air**: Madrid-Barajas Airport (MAD) is the main international airport, well-connected to major cities worldwide.
- **By Train**: Madrid has two main railway stations: Chamartín and Atocha. The AVE high-speed train connects Madrid with Barcelona, Seville, Toledo, Valencia, Alicante, and Málaga.
- **By Road**: Several historic surrounding cities, such as Segovia, Ávila, and Cuenca, are connected by highways.

## Conference Venue

The conference is hosted by **Universidad Rey Juan Carlos**.

The conference will be held at the **Madrid Campus** of the Universidad Rey Juan Carlos, located in the old village of Vicálvaro. This campus is easily accessible by public transport from the city center.
